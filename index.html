FC Barcelona performance story

From possession to pressure points: a connected football story

This prototype turns four isolated analyses into one decision flow: does Barcelona's style still work, when does it break down, which players fit the plan, and how does the club compare with elite rivals?

Narrative spine

One audience, one question chain, one visual language

Act 1

Possession is not the same as control

Start with the myth the club believes in. This view shows whether high possession actually leads to wins, with special attention to away matches.

Why this flow works

Opening with a tension point creates a reason to keep reading. If possession alone is unreliable, the audience naturally asks what causes the failures and how to respond.

Act 2

If the style is fragile, who can sustain it?

Replace the cluttered multi-line design with direct labels and player filtering. This shifts the scouting visual from “many lines” to “decision support.”

Design improvement

Peer feedback highlighted ranking ambiguity and overcrowding. This version uses a ranked list with line trends so growth and current value stay visible at the same time.

Act 3

Which opponents actually shut Barcelona down?

Keep the heatmap, but fix the colour logic and sort the rows to make low-scoring opponents pop out immediately.

Design improvement

Low output is encoded with lighter tones and high output with darker, warmer tones, following a more intuitive “more = darker” convention.

Act 4

Where does Barcelona sit among Europe’s elite?

Finish with benchmarking so the audience leaves with positioning and a recommendation, not just isolated findings.

Why this should be the final view

A benchmark plot works best as a resolution panel. It answers whether the earlier patterns are unique to Barcelona or common among top clubs.
